🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Falcon Drums. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website falcondrums.com and make purchases from us.

This policy complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) in Canada and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union.

Quick Summary: We collect only the information necessary to process your orders and provide customer service. We never sell your personal information to third parties. You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you make a purchase or contact us, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Shipping Address: Street address, city, province/state, postal code, country
  • Order Information: Products purchased, order value, order date
  • Communication Records: Messages you send us via email or contact forms

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Website, we may automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, navigation paths
  • IP Address: For security and fraud prevention
  • Cookies: As described in our Cookie Policy

2.3 Payment Information

Important: We do not store your credit card or payment information on our servers. All payment transactions are processed securely through PayPal, which is PCI-DSS compliant. PayPal handles your payment details according to their own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

3.1 Order Fulfillment

  • Processing and shipping your orders
  • Sending order confirmations and shipping notifications
  • Generating shipping labels and tracking information
  • Managing returns, exchanges, or refunds

3.2 Customer Service

  • Responding to your inquiries and support requests
  • Resolving disputes and troubleshooting problems
  • Providing product information and assistance

3.3 Business Operations

  • Improving our products and services
  • Analyzing Website usage and performance
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security
  • Complying with legal obligations

3.4 Marketing (with consent)

  • Sending promotional emails about new products (you can opt out at any time)
  • Notifying you about special offers or events

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, we process your data based on:

  • Contract Performance: Processing orders and fulfilling purchases
  • Legitimate Interest: Improving our services, fraud prevention, security
  • Consent: Marketing communications and non-essential cookies
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with tax and financial regulations

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business:

  • PayPal: Payment processing (Privacy Policy)
  • EasyPost: Shipping and fulfillment (Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare: Website hosting and security (Privacy Policy)
  • Shipping Carriers: Canada Post, UPS, FedEx, USPS for delivery

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal processes or government requests
  • Enforce our terms and conditions
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevent fraud or illegal activity

5.3 Business Transfers

If Falcon Drums is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you of any such change and your options.

6. Cookie Usage

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our Website. For detailed information about the cookies we use and how to manage them, please see our Cookie Policy.

Cookie Summary:

  • Essential cookies for payment processing (PayPal)
  • Cookie consent preference storage
  • You can manage cookies through our consent banner or browser settings

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted to/from our Website uses SSL/TLS encryption
  • Secure Storage: Data stored in secure, encrypted databases (Cloudflare D1)
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular Security Audits: Ongoing monitoring and security assessments
  • Payment Security: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through PayPal

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Order Information: 7 years (for tax and accounting purposes)
  • Customer Inquiries: 2 years after resolution
  • Marketing Consent: Until you withdraw consent or 2 years of inactivity
  • Website Analytics: Aggregated and anonymized after 90 days

After these periods, we securely delete or anonymize your information.

9. Your Privacy Rights

9.1 Rights Under PIPEDA (Canada)

As a Canadian resident, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for marketing or non-essential processing
  • Complaint: File a complaint with the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

9.2 Rights Under GDPR (European Union)

If you are in the EU, you have additional rights:

  • Right to Access: Obtain confirmation and a copy of your data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

9.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at:

  • Email: info@falcondrums.com
  • Subject Line: "Privacy Rights Request"

We will respond to your request within 30 days (or as required by applicable law). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

10. International Data Transfers

Falcon Drums is based in Canada. If you are accessing our Website from outside Canada, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Canada or other countries where our service providers operate.

For EU residents, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Service providers with adequate data protection certifications
  • Compliance with GDPR requirements for third-country transfers

11. Children's Privacy

Our Website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information from our systems.

12. Marketing Communications

With your consent, we may send you marketing emails about:

  • New products and drum designs
  • Special offers and promotions
  • Company news and updates

Opting Out

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the "Unsubscribe" link in any marketing email
  • Emailing us at info@falcondrums.com
  • Contacting us through the methods listed in Section 14

Note: Even if you opt out of marketing, we will still send you transactional emails related to your orders (confirmations, shipping updates, etc.).

13. Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., social media, payment processors). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or business operations. We will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice on our Website
  • Obtain your consent for material changes where required by law

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

  • "Personal Information" - Information that identifies you as an individual or relates to an identifiable person
