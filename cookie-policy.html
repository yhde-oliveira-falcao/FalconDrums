1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Falcon Drums ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at falcondrums.com (the "Website"). This policy provides you with clear and comprehensive information about the cookies we use and the purposes for using them.

Important: By continuing to use our Website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

3. How We Use Cookies

Falcon Drums uses cookies for the following purposes:

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Website to function properly and cannot be disabled. They are used for:

  • Payment Processing: Enabling secure payment transactions through PayPal
  • Security: Protecting against fraud and ensuring secure connections
  • Cookie Consent: Remembering your cookie preferences

3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization but are not essential to the basic operation of the Website.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that may set their own cookies on your device:

5.1 PayPal Payment Processing

When you use our checkout system, PayPal sets cookies to:

  • Process your payment securely
  • Prevent fraud and protect your transaction
  • Maintain your session during the checkout process
  • Comply with financial regulations

PayPal's use of cookies is governed by their own privacy policy. You can learn more at: PayPal Privacy Policy

Note: PayPal cookies are essential for payment processing. If you decline these cookies, you will not be able to complete purchases on our Website.

6. Your Cookie Choices

You have several options for managing cookies:

6.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our Website, you'll see a cookie consent banner with the following options:

  • Accept All: Allow all cookies including essential and functional cookies
  • Essential Only: Allow only cookies necessary for basic Website functionality
  • Decline: Decline all non-essential cookies (payment processing may be limited)

6.2 Changing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by:

  1. Clearing your browser cookies and revisiting our Website
  2. Using your browser's cookie management settings
  3. Contacting us at info@falcondrums.com

6.3 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Clear cookies when you close your browser

Please note that blocking all cookies may prevent you from using certain features of our Website.

7. Legal Basis for Using Cookies

7.1 GDPR (European Union)

If you are located in the European Union, we process cookies based on:

  • Consent: For non-essential cookies, we obtain your explicit consent
  • Legitimate Interest: For essential cookies required for payment processing and security

7.2 PIPEDA (Canada)

As a Canadian business, we comply with PIPEDA by:

  • Obtaining consent for cookie use through our banner
  • Providing clear information about cookie purposes
  • Allowing you to withdraw consent at any time
  • Using cookies only for legitimate business purposes

8. Data Retention

Cookies we set directly are stored for the following periods:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser

  • Third-Party Cookies: Controlled by respective third-party policies
